The following materials were drawn from two introductory GIS workshops given at MIT during IAP 2016 ![](/images/educator/icon-question-iap.png). They cover the basics of GIS, including data types, metadata, data sources, various GIS software options, multi-step GIS analysis, and automation.

*Note: QGIS is a free and open source Geographic Information System maintained by the* {{% resource_link "22ca986f-9f27-443a-8c91-82bf42e2316c" "*QGIS Project*" %}} *and is available to OCW users.* {{% resource_link "e640078d-5394-4391-9a6c-19923ab4755e" "*ArcGIS*" %}} *is paid software available to the MIT community but not freely available to OCW users.*
